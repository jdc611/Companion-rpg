# Lings — Dracling transformation design (approved November 2026)

## Canonical life stages
All baby Draclings hatch from eggs and are called **Hatchlings**. All mature Draclings are **Elders**. A universal intermediate-stage term is still to be finalized; **Drake** is a leading suggestion, NOT approved yet. Pattern: Hatchling → [intermediate term] → Elder.

## Branching structure
Every three-stage Ling species has one baby/base form, two intermediate forms (offense and defense), and two mature forms continuing those branches: five illustrated designs total. Physical branch is permanent; combat specialization (Striker, Bruiser, Tank, Caster, Support) is separate and can be respecified. Other species may have unique branch names. Reserve **Guardian** for the ancient eight Guardians, not regular transformations.

## First Dracling visual direction (user approved)
The shared Hatchling is cute, orange-red, blue markings, cream frilled ears, little wings, and expressive eyes.
- **Elemental — offensive lineage:** intermediate is a visibly adolescent long, slim, somewhat lanky dragon with exaggerated oversized still-developing wings, youthful head, early blue elemental markings; it can glide but not sustain full flight. Mature Elder Elemental has huge fully functional wings, elegant streamlined body, more extensive elemental markings and mature head/horns; sustained flight.
- **Bulwark — defensive lineage:** intermediate is stocky, broad-chested, strong-legged, adolescent, with **no natural armor plating yet**; mature Elder Bulwark develops heavy natural protective plating on shoulders, back, and limbs, broader durable body.
- Intermediate forms must NOT look like scaled-down elders. Their juvenile anatomy and lack of final-stage signature traits should be immediately recognizable. Both branches retain shared ancestry in orange-red, cream, blue color language.
