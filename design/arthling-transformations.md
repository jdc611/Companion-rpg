# Arthling family — approved initial design direction

## Life stages (approved)
**Grub → Strider → Prime**. These are universal Arthling life-stage labels, distinct from Dracling **Hatchling → Drake → Elder**. Grub is a fantasy stage label and does not require wormlike anatomy.

## First ordinary Arthling (concept; art not yet finalized)
- **Grub:** cute ant-inspired baby with six legs, segmented body, curved antennae, tiny mandibles, oversized expressive eyes; keep arthropod, not dragon anatomy.
- **Offensive lineage:** gradually develops bee/wasp-inspired anatomy, narrower waist, stinger, and developing wings in Strider stage; Prime gains fully developed wings and offensive stinger.
- **Defensive lineage:** gradually develops spider-inspired low broad stance, thick legs, larger abdomen, web/silk motifs; potentially grows a fourth pair of legs by Prime. Avoid beetle-like heavy armor.
- Shared warm amber, charcoal, subtle teal visual ancestry; offensive branch may be more golden, defensive branch darker.
- Interesting future gameplay idea: colony-related coordination/summoning helpers. Not yet implemented; focus currently on visual design.

## Ancient Arthling distinction
Ancient Guardian Arthling already combines stag beetle head, praying mantis body, dragonfly wings, forehead Council Sigil. Do not duplicate those distinctive features in ordinary first Arthling.

## Design principle
Intermediate Strider should look adolescent and visibly different from fully developed Prime, not merely a smaller adult. Each family has species-specific offense/defense branch names, not necessarily Elemental/Bulwark.
