# LINGS — CREATURE DESIGN MASTER
Updated November 4, 2026. This is the canonical design decision record. Concept art generated in ChatGPT conversation is visual reference; the images are not yet uploaded to the repo as production assets.

## Global design system
Eight families: Bestiling, Dracling, Morphling, Florling, Arthling, Aquiling, Terraling, Spectraling. Species are separate from family and life-stage names. Three life stages per species: one base, two offense/defense intermediate forms, two corresponding mature forms (five designs per species). Middle stage must be visibly adolescent; mature form gains signature features, not merely size. Physical lineage is permanent. Combat specialties (working set: Striker, Bruiser, Tank, Caster, Support) are separate and potentially respecable. Affinity is separate from family, species, lineage, specialty; each species has a dominant native affinity and rare location-dependent mutation affinities. All affinities use EXACTLY the same artwork and animations. Potential affinity stat tradeoffs and elemental matchups to be balanced later; rare variants are not automatically better. Guardian refers to the eight Ancient Guardians, never a regular combat form.

## FLORLING — VERDANT (APPROVED DESIGN DIRECTION)
User approved the revised five-form Florling artwork on November 4, 2026. **Universal Florling life stages: Sprout → Bloom → Flourish**, approved. **Verdant** is the current species name shown in the approved art; name has not been separately explicitly locked. Botanical colony concept, NOT a single giant humanoid plant, to distinguish from Ancient Florling (a large mobile jungle organism that can root and expand, with head Council Sigil).
1. **Sprout (base):** adorable small living grassy patch, vivid lime-green grass blades, dark shadowed face with two glowing yellow eyes peeking through, tiny white flowers. Low, ground-hugging silhouette.
2. **Offense Bloom:** several (about three) tall slender flower stems grow from one shared grassy patch, each pink-petaled flower has its own expressive dark face and yellow eyes. Distinct coordinated individuals, but **one Ling**.
3. **Offense Flourish:** more numerous, taller, more elaborate flowering stems (roughly 3–5) with individual expressive faces, pink/gold petals and coordinated colony identity. Potential multihit attacks because individual flowers strike in sequence.
4. **Defense Bloom:** grassy patch spreads into a small network of individually animated thorny vines; multiple curved vine tips with little eyes/faces, brambles beginning to form. No central humanoid creature.
5. **Defense Flourish:** sprawling living bramble colony, many independent thick thorny vine bodies and expressive tips, coordinated defensive entanglement and regeneration. No single central head or giant humanoid trunk.
Reference: second revised Florling infographic (November 4, 2026), featuring Petalhost offense and Thornbrood defense; user said 'I like it.' Petalhost/Thornbrood are **working mature-form labels**, not independently finalized. Shared bright grassy base; offense pink flower heads; defense green vines with red thorns. A colony is one combatant/Ling.

## Species stage-count flexibility (APPROVED)
Not all species need 3 stages. A species may have a single mature form (e.g. only Flourish/Alpha), two stages skipping the intermediate (e.g. Sprout → Flourish), or the full three-stage progression. Stage structure is species-defined, not random per individual. The first Verdant concept uses all three stages/two lineages; this does not force other species to do so.
