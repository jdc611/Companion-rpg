# LINGS — CREATURE DESIGN MASTER
Updated November 4, 2026. This is the canonical design decision record. Concept art generated in ChatGPT conversation is visual reference; the images are not yet uploaded to the repo as production assets.

## Global design system
Eight families: Bestiling, Dracling, Morphling, Florling, Arthling, Aquiling, Terraling, Spectraling. Species are separate from family and life-stage names. Three life stages per species: one base, two offense/defense intermediate forms, two corresponding mature forms (five designs per species). Middle stage must be visibly adolescent; mature form gains signature features, not merely size. Physical lineage is permanent. Combat specialties (working set: Striker, Bruiser, Tank, Caster, Support) are separate and potentially respecable. Affinity is separate from family, species, lineage, specialty; each species has a dominant native affinity and rare location-dependent mutation affinities. All affinities use EXACTLY the same artwork and animations. Potential affinity stat tradeoffs and elemental matchups to be balanced later; rare variants are not automatically better. Guardian refers to the eight Ancient Guardians, never a regular combat form.

5. **Defense Alpha (lion):** huge regal muscular lion, full flowing golden/brown mane, broad chest, powerful paws, imposing protective silhouette. **Keep latest design as is**.

## MORPHLING — MORFEX (VISUAL DESIGN APPROVED / LOCKED)
**Species name: Morfex.** User explicitly loved and approved the latest five-form concept artwork on November 4, 2026. Purple/violet and cyan palette, large expressive cyan eyes, small cute two-legged base form, two connected arms, pliable organic matter. No aquatic/amphibian/tentacle emphasis; reserve those for Aquilings. Do not duplicate Ancient Morphling's detached floating arms and forehead Council Sigil.
1. **Base:** adorable small round soft-bodied purple-and-lavender creature with cyan eyes and luminous cyan seams, short connected arms and legs, playful face and swept-back pliable crest.
2. **Offensive intermediate:** lean, agile, more angular adolescent with developing pointed forelimbs and an elongated swept-back crest; visibly less elaborate than adult.
3. **Offensive mature:** elegant upright athletic morphic fighter with large cyan-glowing elongated blade-like forearms, sharp flowing purple crest, streamlined silhouette. Blades are its own reshaped tissue, not equipment.
4. **Defensive intermediate:** broader stocky adolescent, enlarged hands/forearms, lower stance, thick but flexible body.
5. **Defensive mature:** massive broad-shouldered organic protector with huge flexible arms and hands, body can temporarily harden/reshape to protect. The generated image looks somewhat armored; user approved overall visual, but earlier direction specifically favors **organic elastic anatomy, not worn or permanent hard armor**.
**Reference:** most recent Morphling infographic in November 4, 2026 ChatGPT conversation, showing title 'Morfex', with purple/cyan five-form evolution. It is not yet uploaded to repository as an image. Labels on image: Sprout → Flux → Apex; separately proposed Mote → Shifter → Paragon. **Universal Morphling stage names remain UNDECIDED**; do not lock either set without user choice. ## FLORLING — VERDANT (APPROVED DESIGN DIRECTION)
User approved the revised five-form Florling artwork on November 4, 2026. **Universal Florling life stages: Sprout → Bloom → Flourish**, approved. **Verdant** is the current species name shown in the approved art; name has not been separately explicitly locked. Botanical colony concept, NOT a single giant humanoid plant, to distinguish from Ancient Florling (a large mobile jungle organism that can root and expand, with head Council Sigil).
1. **Sprout (base):** adorable small living grassy patch, vivid lime-green grass blades, dark shadowed face with two glowing yellow eyes peeking through, tiny white flowers. Low, ground-hugging silhouette.
2. **Offense Bloom:** several (about three) tall slender flower stems grow from one shared grassy patch, each pink-petaled flower has its own expressive dark face and yellow eyes. Distinct coordinated individuals, but **one Ling**.
3. **Offense Flourish:** more numerous, taller, more elaborate flowering stems (roughly 3–5) with individual expressive faces, pink/gold petals and coordinated colony identity. Potential multihit attacks because individual flowers strike in sequence.
4. **Defense Bloom:** grassy patch spreads into a small network of individually animated thorny vines; multiple curved vine tips with little eyes/faces, brambles beginning to form. No central humanoid creature.
5. **Defense Flourish:** sprawling living bramble colony, many independent thick thorny vine bodies and expressive tips, coordinated defensive entanglement and regeneration. No single central head or giant humanoid trunk.
Reference: second revised Florling infographic (November 4, 2026), featuring Petalhost offense and Thornbrood defense; user said 'I like it.' Petalhost/Thornbrood are **working mature-form labels**, not independently finalized. Shared bright grassy base; offense pink flower heads; defense green vines with red thorns. A colony is one combatant/Ling.

## Species stage-count flexibility (APPROVED)
Not all species need 3 stages. A species may have a single mature form (e.g. only Flourish/Alpha), two stages skipping the intermediate (e.g. Sprout → Flourish), or the full three-stage progression. Stage structure is species-defined, not random per individual. The first Verdant concept uses all three stages/two lineages; this does not force other species to do so.

## AQUILING — LIFE STAGES AND FIRST TWO SPECIES (NOVEMBER 4, 2026)
**Universal Aquiling stages officially approved: Fry → Shoal → Leviathan.** The stage labels apply across Aquilings even if an individual species has only one or two stages.

### Aquiling species 1: Tidefin (artwork direction user likes)
User explicitly liked the first illustrated Tidefin concept sheet: blue/white amphibious four-legged aquatic creature, glowing cyan fins, broad tail, big expressive eyes; offensive line is sleek and finned, defensive line is broad with shell-like plating. Retain this as the first Aquiling's approved visual direction despite assistant earlier suggesting it might be too dragon-like. **Important:** the illustration's labels Sprout/Surge/Bastion/Riptide/Aegis are NOT canonical life-stage names; canonical stages are Fry/Shoal/Leviathan. 'Tidefin' is a working species name from art, not separately confirmed. Detailed moves/affinities unfinalized.

### Aquiling species 2: fish → shark offense / whale defense (concept agreed, artwork pending)
- Base Fry: small rounded expressive true fish, fins and swimming silhouette, not four-legged.
- Offensive Shoal: young sleek shark-inspired fish, emerging dorsal fin, sharper teeth, still juvenile.
- Offensive Leviathan: mature streamlined formidable shark predator, dramatic distinct adult anatomy.
- Defensive Shoal: broad plump juvenile fish developing whale-like proportions.
- Defensive Leviathan: massive smooth-bodied whale-inspired protector with powerful horizontal tail fluke and blowhole, unmistakably cetacean; **avoid** Ancient Aquiling's walrus/seal body, tusks, whiskers and flippers-only silhouette.
- Second species name and visual art have NOT yet been chosen or approved. Stage count may vary by species under the global rule.

### Ancient Aquiling
Immortal Ancient/Guardian Aquiling is walrus/seal inspired with enormous tusks, whiskers, and flippers, Council Sigil on forehead. Ordinary Aquilings should not duplicate this signature anatomy.

## TERRALING — PRISMITE (NOVEMBER 4, 2026)
**Universal Terraling life stages approved: Shard → Prism → Monolith.**

### First ordinary Terraling species: Prismite (latest concept positively received)
The user liked the revised non-animal crystalline design, specifically avoiding the overused sleek quadruped offensive silhouette and armored turtle defensive silhouette. Latest concept art shown in ChatGPT: title “Terraling: Prismite,” purple/icy-cyan crystal palette, glowing eyes/energy core, dark crystal cavern, two dramatically distinct paths. User said “That one’s cool.” Treat as favored design direction, not necessarily final locked artwork or name.
- **Shard base:** adorable small levitating irregular living crystal cluster, bright eyes in shadowy central face, violet and icy cyan crystal shards and tiny floating fragments.
- **Offense Prism:** compact levitating glowing central core with numerous independently orbiting sharp crystal blades, geometric rings/resonance, no animal limbs or body.
- **Offense Monolith:** spectacular large suspended crystal core surrounded by wide halo and many floating elongated crystal blades arranged like a mobile orbital weapon system; can launch shards and reassemble.
- **Defense Prism:** growing central crystalline core with upright separated rock/crystal panels arranged in a protective ring, beginning to resemble a geode.
- **Defense Monolith:** monumental stationary hollow geode sanctuary, interlocking vertical mineral pillars and crystal walls protecting a glowing inner core, geometric orbiting energy rings; not a turtle, not a quadruped, not an Ancient Terraling stone humanoid.
- Distinction: offense projects crystals outward; defense builds a sanctuary inward. No need for limbs, animal face, conventional body, or shell.
- Provisional branch names from concept art: Resonance (offense), Bastion (defense); species name Prismite featured in art, user reacted positively but has not explicitly confirmed as final.
- Keep elemental affinities independent of physical forms, per global rules. Don't lock image-generated game mechanics without discussion.
